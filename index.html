How to escape

Every turnOne card burns face-down, one card is drawn, then you swipe one card from the six-card hand into discard. The next burn and draw happen automatically.
KeysAny 7 or the Red Joker completes an eligible out hand. Keys are not wild in Standard mode.
Trap 2s & 5sIf a 2 or 5 is drawn it must stay for that turn. Swipe a different non-trap card. Trap cards can be discarded on a later normal turn.
Black JokerDestroys the hand. Your cards go to discard and the Black Joker goes to burn. You then rebuild the hand from the deck.
Red JokerSwipe it into discard to reshuffle the remaining deck and burn pile together. Discards stay gone. If Black Joker is drawn while Red Joker is held, both cancel each other and the rest of the hand survives.
Standard gameYou have up to three runs. Complete an out hand during any run to escape.
